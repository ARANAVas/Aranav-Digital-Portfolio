    04 / MY STORY

    A curious mind.
    A growing craft.

    I came to marketing through curiosity and practical work. I enjoy understanding why a message connects, who it is meant for and what would make the next step clearer.

    Studying Physics at Daudnagar College has shaped how I approach problems: look at the information, notice patterns and work through the question instead of guessing. I bring that mindset to marketing — understanding an audience, sharpening a message and choosing a practical way to reach people.

    Through IT Hub Solution and RICOZ Innovation, I built experience in SEO fundamentals, social content, website reviews and day-to-day campaign support. At EliteFinish, I took on broader marketing responsibilities — from content calendars and website checks to creative coordination and team follow-through.

    I’m still building my experience, and I’m comfortable saying that. My focus is to learn by doing, take responsibility for the details and become someone a team can rely on. Over time, I want to help build brands through clear communication and consistent work.
