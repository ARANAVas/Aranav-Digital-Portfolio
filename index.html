    04 / MY STORY

    From Physics
    to digital marketing.

    I’ve always wanted to understand how things work. That curiosity is what led me from Physics towards digital marketing.

    As a B.Sc. Physics student at Daudnagar College, I spend time asking questions, finding patterns and working through problems. I brought the same curiosity to digital platforms, learning SEO, social media, content planning and the practical work behind campaigns.

    At IT Hub Solution and RICOZ Innovation, I built my foundation in digital marketing. At EliteFinish, I took on a wider role as Head of Marketing — planning content, reviewing the website, coordinating creative work and keeping projects moving.

    I like a clear brief, a strong idea and the care it takes to finish well. I’m still learning, and I take every project seriously.
