SITE INFORMATION / PRIVACY

Privacy, plainly.

This is a personal portfolio website. It does not provide user accounts, collect contact-form submissions on a server, or run an analytics or advertising tracker in this release.

Contact message

The contact form validates the information in your browser and creates an email draft addressed to aranavprime@gmail.com. Your email application opens the draft; you review it and choose whether to send it. The website itself does not transmit or store the form fields.

Theme preference

The dark/light theme preference may be stored locally in your browser using local storage. It is not sent to the site owner.

External resources

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser makes requests to Google’s font servers when those fonts are needed; Google’s handling of request data is governed by its own privacy terms.

Hosting

The website is intended for static hosting. The hosting provider may process standard technical request data, such as IP address and request logs, under its own policies.
